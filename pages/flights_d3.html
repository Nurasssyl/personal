✈️ Overview of Direct International Flights from Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan currently has 12 international airports offering direct flights abroad. These routes connect the country to 31 different countries across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East—highlighting Kazakhstan’s growing role in regional air travel.

Top Airports by Connectivity

Among all the airports, Almaty leads by the number of international cities it connects with, followed closely by Astana. These two major hubs serve as Kazakhstan’s gateways to the world. Regional airports such as Shymkent, Atyrau, and Aktau also contribute to the international network by providing access to strategic destinations.

Analytical Highlights

  • Almaty offers the most international routes by city count.
  • Astana reaches nearly as many countries, reflecting its national importance.
  • Turkistan and Kostanay serve only a few destinations, often seasonal or niche routes.
  • Reverse fact: Some countries only connect with Almaty or Astana — leaving regional cities underused.
  • Russia, Turkey, UAE, Georgia, and China are among the most frequently served destinations.

These patterns reflect both demand-driven routes and geopolitical realities, offering insight for travelers, airlines, and policymakers alike.

Interesting Facts

  • Russia tops the list, with direct flights from Kazakhstan to 11 different Russian cities.
  • China and Turkey follow with 6 and 5 cities respectively, reflecting close regional ties.
  • Kyzylorda and Oskemen each serve only one foreign country, making them the most specialized.
  • Turkey, Egypt, Russia, and Georgia are connected to Kazakhstan by 5 or more airports, proving their popularity and accessibility.

As air travel continues to recover and expand, these patterns will evolve—potentially opening new routes from emerging Kazakh cities.
