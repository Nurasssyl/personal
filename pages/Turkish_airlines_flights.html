Turkish Airlines Destinations

✈️ Mapping Turkish Airlines' Global Reach

This visualization highlights the international network of Turkish Airlines — the global leader by number of countries served. The airline connects cities across all continents, forming a unique map of global accessibility.

Facts and Coverage

As of April 2025, Turkish Airlines operates scheduled flights to 270 international and 52 domestic destinations in 131 countries, including hubs, cargo routes, and seasonal destinations.

While Istanbul is the main hub, international departures are also operated from Ankara, Antalya, and Izmir, extending the reach and reducing pressure on a single hub.

Key Observations

  • Most dense coverage is seen across Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa.
  • Routes continue to expand, especially across the Asia-Pacific region.
  • Some countries have a single destination, while others (e.g. Germany, USA) host dozens of connections.
  • This map is built from destination coordinates and arc routes from Istanbul, simulating real flight corridors.

The layout reveals both geographic strategy and broader priorities — transit, tourism, business, and migration flows.

🔴 Turkish Airlines continues to serve as a vital global bridge between East and West.
